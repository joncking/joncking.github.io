I'm Jonathan King, an AI/ML engineer based in Raleigh, NC. I build agentic and ML systems to solve business problems. On the side, I enjoy working on problems related to neuroscience, mechanistic interpretability, and whatever interesting paper I've read most recently

I previously worked on canine neuroimaging research using awake fMRI, contributing to studies on odor decoding and visual processing in dog brains. 

## Areas of Interest

- **Alignment & Mechanistic Interpretability** -- Understanding and improving how AI systems behave, represent information, and align with human intentions
- **Automation for ML & Agentic Systems** -- Building tools and workflows that make building, evaluating, and utilizing models and agents easier and more effective
- **Clinical Neuroscience & AI/ML** -- Utilizing AI/ML methods for clinical neuroscience and neuroimaging analysis
